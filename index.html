The slot machine deals you a team and a decade. Pick one player, slot him in. Five rounds, five positions, five different eras. Build a lineup good enough to go undefeated.

How to play
  • Each round, a random decade and franchise are rolled.
  • Pick exactly one player and place him at an open court position he actually played.
  • Fill all five spots: PG, SG, SF, PF, C — each from a different decade.
  • You get one team skip and one era skip per game.
  • After five picks, your season is simulated into a record from 0–82 to 82–0.
